REGINA DATA MAP
A free tool from Franklin Kinetics, Regina. franklinkinetics.com

HOW TO OPEN IT
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click "regina-data-map.html". It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).
3. That's it. It works on a phone too: save the file, then open it from your Files app.

No account, no install, and it doesn't need internet once you have the file. (Without internet it uses your device's own fonts, so it looks a little different.)

WHAT TO TRY
- Pick a story on the left: "The $10 million club", "Suites and basements", "What's new this month".
- Press play to watch a year of Regina building permits appear on the map.
- Click "Surprise me".
- Shade the map by zoning, income, housing cost or building per person.
- Click any dot or any neighbourhood.

HOW FRESH IS IT
Built on {built}. Building permits run through {permits_through}. The City of Regina updates its permit list every Thursday, so newer permits are not in your copy. The map shows its own "data as of" dates in the About tab.
For the latest version, come back to the product page at franklinkinetics.com and download again.

WHERE THE DATA COMES FROM (government sources only)
- City of Regina open data: building permits, zoning, streets, addresses, proposed development notices.
  Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence - City of Regina.
- Statistics Canada: population and migration, and the 2021 Census of Population.
  Adapted from Statistics Canada, Tables 17-10-0155-01 and 17-10-0149-01 and the 2021 Census of Population. This does not constitute an endorsement by Statistics Canada of this product.
- Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation: Rental Market Survey (rents and vacancy).

GOOD TO KNOW
- A permit means work is approved to begin. It doesn't mean it is finished or that it will cost the amount declared.
- The income and housing-cost shading is an estimate. The Census doesn't publish those figures for Regina's community associations, so they are worked out from the census areas that overlap each one.
- Rezonings are spotted by comparing the City's zoning maps over time, and only show changes since 26 July 2026.
- No company, applicant or owner names are in this map. It is about places and projects, not people.
