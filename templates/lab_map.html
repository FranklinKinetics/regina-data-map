How to read this map

What a building permit is

The City issues a permit before most construction, demolition or major renovation can start. Each dot is one permit at the property it covers. A permit means the work is approved to begin. It does not mean the work is finished, or that it will go ahead on the declared budget.

What "declared value" means

The applicant's own estimate of construction cost when they apply. It is not an appraisal, a sale price or the final cost. Some projects are declared in stages, so one building can appear in more than one permit.

Zoning, rezonings and open applications

Zoning sets what can be built on land. "Urban holding" land is set aside for future development and usually needs a rezoning first. Diamonds mark places where the City's zoning map changed since our first snapshot. Circles are applications the City currently lists on its Proposed Development page; orange ones are open for public comment.

Income, housing-cost and home-value shading

Home value is the median value owners gave for their own home in the 2021 Census. It is not a City assessment or a sale price, and 2021 values are older than today's market. These come from the 2021 Census (Statistics Canada). The Census does not publish figures for community associations, so each figure is an estimate built from the census blocks that overlap each area. Treat it as a good guide, not a precise count.
